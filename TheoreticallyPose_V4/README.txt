TheoreticallyPose V4 — quick start
==================================

1. Double-click TheoreticallyPose_V4.html  (use Chrome or Edge)
2. Drag a video in — try the included sample_clip.mp4
3. Hit Track, then Bake, then Export MP4

That's it. Full guide: open TheoreticallyPose_V4_Guide.html (or the PDF).

Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing gets uploaded.
The depth models (~59 MB) download automatically on first Bake and are
cached after that.
